## The Weekly Blog Posts From BeingKevin

### Sunday — A Book That Is Difficult to Forget

We finished the week with a look at _Hidden Valley Road: Inside the Mind of an American Family_ by Robert Kolker.

This is not exactly a light weekend read. The book follows one extraordinary American family in which six of twelve children developed schizophrenia. But what makes Kolker's book so compelling is that it is about much more than mental illness. It is about family, science, guilt, survival, and what happens when a family is forced to live with something nobody completely understands.

I found it heartbreaking, fascinating, and ultimately very human. If you enjoy books that leave you thinking about them long after you have finished reading, this one deserves a look.
